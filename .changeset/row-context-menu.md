**Row actions where the row is.** Right-clicking a repository, pull-request, issue or notification row
in the sidebar, a saved instance or AI endpoint row on the settings page, or a suggestion card in the AI
pre-review panel now shows that surface's own VS Code-native items — open it, copy its address or
number, open it in the browser, mark a notification as read, edit or remove an instance, test an
endpoint, copy a suggested comment's body. The editor's cut/copy/paste menu no longer appears where it
had nothing to act on, and selecting text in a job log offers Copy; fields and selected text keep the
platform menu.
